# Inputs for the fixed surface reconstruction programme

This ledger accompanies [R1 of the reconstruction plan](../milestones/GWReconstructionPlan.md#r1-specify-the-three-targets-and-all-geometric-inputs). It fixes the distinction between algebra proved in Lean and geometric hypotheses still needed for reconstruction. The targets remain $X_0=\mathbb P^2$, $X_1=\mathbb F_1$, and $X_2=\mathrm{Bl}_{p,q}\mathbb P^2$; the benchmark bounds are unchanged.

## Algebra and curve coordinates

[`SurfaceCohomology`](../../AxiomaticGW/Surfaces/Cohomology.lean) constructs the rational algebra with unit, hyperplane, exceptional, and point coordinates. Its trace is the point coefficient. Its product and pairing use $H^2=\mathrm{pt}$, $E_iE_j=-\delta_{ij}\mathrm{pt}$, and $HE_i=0$. This algebraic construction does not prove a comparison with geometric cohomology.

[`SurfaceTarget`](../../AxiomaticGW/Surfaces/CurveClasses.lean) fixes the three effective coordinate types. The change to signed $(H,E_i)$ lattice coordinates is additive and injective, and the first-Chern degree agrees with intersection against $3H-\sum E_i$. [`WeightedCurveClass`](../../AxiomaticGW/Coefficients/WeightedCurveClass.lean) supplies executable bounded sets and ordered splittings, with equality proofs against the existing `EffectiveCurveMonoid` sets. Kernel-checked tests give $5,49,455$ classes, including zero, through energy twelve.

Geometric interpretation must identify **all** effective numerical classes with these monoids. In particular, a statement about irreducible curves cannot exclude reducible effective cycles. For example, $2E+F=H+E$ is an effective class on $\mathbb F_1$ with positive exceptional coefficient in the $(H,E)$ basis. It is retained by the coordinate model. Any vanishing needed by reconstruction must be proved or explicitly sourced separately.
